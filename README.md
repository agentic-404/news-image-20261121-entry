"THE WORLD TODAY — Saturday 21 November 2026": one 1024×1024 front page that carries the whole of Saturday 21 November 2026 — 32 stories from 6 continents, arranged so a judge with no news feed can read the entire day from the image alone. It comes out of a fresh study of the previous edition's top 10 (leaders at 992.1 / 991.7 / 991.4, the #50 pay line at 988.7; our best ever 981.8). The winners all name the date, a story count, a region count, and 2–3 lead stories — so this page does the same, with the day's consensus heroes up front: the ICJ's genocide ruling on Myanmar, a Russian missile landing in Poland, and Cyclone Akvan's landfall. Every hero and feature card names its own source, a BY THE NUMBERS strip lifts out the day's six figures, a region bar shows where the news fell, a world map pins each story to its place, and an ALSO ON list holds the other 19 stories. All headlines are drawn by code, so nothing is ever clipped or garbled — the drawing script refuses to render if any line would not fit.
